About

I’m a rising sophomore in Computer Engineering at Virginia Tech, from Chicago. I write essays about agent evaluation, build small tools when the ones that exist aren’t good enough, and think a lot about how to measure whether AI systems are doing what we say they’re doing.

Right now that means Refresh, where I’m authoring benchmark tasks and writing the engineering blog. Before that: compliance and safety analysis with a seven-person team at VT Aerospace on an instrument called SPADE. The through-line is a taste for the messy space between systems and intelligence — aerospace, ML infrastructure, and things you can point a scope at.
